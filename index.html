About

The person behind the work

My career started at the intersection of data and democratic politics — building revenue forecasts and market analyses at ActBlue, managing data for presidential campaigns, and studying how small-dollar donors shape American elections. I care about what data can do to shape peoples' experiences, and work with product teams to make sure we're improving peoples lives, not just our own bottom lines.

Questions around technology development and data privacy are what brought me to policy. I'm finishing an MS in Data Science for Public Policy at Georgetown's McCourt School, focused on technology policy — specifically how governments can build the legal and institutional capacity to hold Big Tech accountable.
